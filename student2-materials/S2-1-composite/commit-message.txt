[S2-1] Composite: product bundles

StoreItem is the common interface for anything that can go into the
cart. ProductItem (leaf) wraps one catalog product, ProductBundle
(composite) holds other store items, including other bundles. A bundle
answers every question by asking its children: the price is the sum of
the children minus the bundle discount, and it is available only when
every child is in stock. Tests cover nesting, prices, availability and
rejecting a bundle that would contain itself.
